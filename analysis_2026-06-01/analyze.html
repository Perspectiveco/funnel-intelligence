How this works — 2-min methodology

We analysed ~196,000 real Perspective funnels (and ~10,000 historical A/B tests) to build three things:

1 · Niche

We embed every funnel's copy with a multilingual model and group them into 32 niches by meaning. A classifier assigns a new funnel to its niche from its text — 92% agreement on funnels from accounts it never saw, and it asks you to confirm when it's unsure.

2 · Score (/100)

A model predicts a funnel's conversion percentile within its niche from its design + copy. Validated honestly: ρ=0.40 on unseen accounts (it generalises, not memorises) and ρ=0.48 predicting newer funnels from older ones (it predicts the future, not just fits the past).

3 · Recommendations

For each niche we measured what the best-converting funnels do differently. Suggestions are split into universal moves (true across niches), niche-specific ones, and a “test, don't apply” bucket — because some patterns (e.g. more form questions) correlate with winning but cause losses in real A/B tests.

The honest caveats

  1. Funnel design explains ~13% of conversion — audience, offer and traffic dominate the rest. The score is a relative design-quality signal, not a CVR oracle.
  2. It's correlational: recommendations are experiments to A/B-test, not guaranteed fixes. The A/B flywheel turns the strong ones into confident, causal advice over time.
  3. Copy is the biggest lever (bigger than layout) — so the highest-value tests are usually messaging, which is why we also generate tailored opener suggestions.
