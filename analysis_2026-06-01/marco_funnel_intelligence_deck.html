Finding 1
87/13

87% of conversion is the niche, offer & audience. Only ~13% is the funnel's design.

So the score is a percentile within a niche — never an absolute number.

Finding 2 — the Funnel Score is real
ρ = 0.48

Design predicts within-niche conversion at 0.48 — past the 0.40 gate.

And the copy matters more than the layout. A zero-traffic score is feasible.
